## Hidden Truths

### The Watch Station Evidence Room is Compromised

The city watch evidence room in the Merchants' Quarter station is not secure. Lieutenant Voss Harren, who oversees evidence management, is on The Architect's payroll. He has already been instructed to "lose" critical pages from Vex's smuggling ledger.

Harren plans to complete the evidence tampering during the shift change tonight (3:00 AM). He will burn the incriminating pages and report that the evidence was damaged by "water infiltration" from a leaking pipe he will personally sabotage.

Captain Marris does not know Harren is compromised. If confronted with evidence of tampering, Marris will immediately arrest Harren, but that requires someone discovering the plot in the next few hours.

---

### The Architect is Councilor Thrane Silvermark

Councilor Thrane Silvermark, a respected member of Ironhaven's city council and a candidate for guildmaster, is The Architect.

Silvermark built the smuggling network over 10 years using his political connections and inherited merchant fortune. He operates through intermediaries and coded messages, never meeting his agents directly. His public persona—principled reformer, advocate for transparent governance—is the opposite of his private reality.

Silvermark orchestrated Vex's assassination to:
1. Eliminate a potential witness
2. Frame the investigators and discredit their evidence
3. Create chaos allowing him to claim guildmaster authority
4. Consolidate control of both city council and merchant guild

He is currently three moves from checkmate. Once he becomes guildmaster, he will control Ironhaven's commercial and political infrastructure.

---

### The Auction House Basement Contains Backup Records

Guildmaster Vex kept backup records of all smuggling transactions hidden in the auction house basement. The records are concealed inside a hollowed-out support pillar (third from the east entrance) behind a false stone panel.

These records include:
- Names of all participants in the smuggling network
- Financial transactions (who profited, how much)
- Coded communications from The Architect
- The cipher key to decrypt all messages
- Evidence implicating Councilor Silvermark

The records are comprehensive enough to bring down the entire operation—if someone finds them before Silvermark's agents do.

Silvermark doesn't know these records exist. Vex never told anyone except the note hidden in his signet ring (currently in watch custody).

---

### The Brass Scales Inn Proprietor is Holding Vex's Final Letter

Aldric Grenn, proprietor of The Brass Scales Inn, is holding a sealed letter from Guildmaster Vex with instructions to deliver it to "whoever investigates my death" exactly 48 hours after Vex's demise.

The letter contains:
- Vex's confession to embezzlement
- His suspicion that Councilor Silvermark is The Architect (based on writing style analysis of coded letters)
- The cipher key for all communications
- The location of the auction house backup records
- An apology and a plea to finish what he couldn't

Grenn will deliver the letter in 24 in-game hours (2 days after the session where Vex died). Grenn is trustworthy and not compromised—he's one of the few genuinely neutral parties in the quarter.

However, if Silvermark learns of the letter's existence, he will send assassins to kill Grenn and destroy it.

## Latent Threats

### The Quarter is One Rumor From Riots

The merchant houses are terrified. The lockdown is strangling commerce. Smaller merchants are days from bankruptcy. Fear of arrest is spreading.

If any of the following occur, the quarter will erupt into riots:
- Another high-profile arrest or assassination
- Extension of the lockdown beyond 7 days
- Watch officers begin confiscating merchant inventories
- Torven Brasscoin publicly accuses the council of orchestrating Vex's death

Riots would mean:
- City watch overwhelmed
- Silvermark declaring martial law (giving him emergency powers)
- Evidence destroyed in fires and looting
- Innocent casualties

The quarter is a powder keg. Any significant spark will ignite it.

---

### Silvermark's Agents are Hunting the Party

Silvermark has placed bounties on Kael and Sera (delivered through criminal intermediaries, untraceable to him):
- 500 gold for their deaths (made to look like escape attempts)
- 1,000 gold for their deaths AND recovery of any evidence they possess

Three groups are currently hunting the party:
1. **The Pale Knives** (assassin guild): Professional, patient, will strike when party is isolated
2. **Brasscoin's Mercenaries** (unknowingly working for Silvermark): Brutal, direct, will attack in public if opportunity arises
3. **Corrupted Watch Officers** (2-3 individuals): Will arrange "accidents" during custody or transport

The party has 48-72 hours before one of these groups succeeds, unless they escape custody or secure protection.

---

### The Lockdown Will Become Permanent if Silvermark Wins

If Silvermark becomes guildmaster while maintaining his council seat, he plans to make the lockdown permanent under different pretexts:
- "Emergency security measures during transition"
- "Investigation into broader criminal conspiracy"
- "Protection of merchant interests from external threats"

Permanent lockdown means:
- Complete control of who enters and leaves the quarter
- Ability to arrest anyone under suspicion without evidence
- Elimination of political rivals under cover of law
- Transformation of the quarter into a police state

This is his endgame: total control masked as legitimate governance.

If Silvermark learns about Vex's backup records or final letter, he will accelerate his timeline and take riskier actions, potentially exposing himself.

## GM Notes

### The Investigation's Real Stakes

This isn't just about clearing the party's names. It's about stopping Silvermark before he achieves permanent control of Ironhaven's commercial and political infrastructure.

The party has stumbled into a conspiracy that's been building for a decade. They're outmatched, outmaneuvered, and operating on a ticking clock. But they're also the only ones who know enough and care enough to stop it.

### Multiple Paths to Victory

1. **Find the backup records** → public exposure → force council investigation
2. **Secure Vex's letter** → decode communications → trace network to Silvermark
3. **Catch evidence tampering** → flip Lieutenant Harren → implicate Silvermark
4. **Survive long enough** → 48-hour letter delivery → cipher key → decode everything
5. **Recruit Captain Marris** → legitimate authority → formal investigation

None of these alone will defeat Silvermark—he's too well-insulated. But combining 2-3 approaches creates enough pressure that his network collapses.

### Silvermark's Mistake

Silvermark's arrogance is his weakness. He believes framing the party for murder and having them executed within 48 hours is sufficient. He's not accounting for:
- Captain Marris's reluctance to rush to judgment
- Vex's dead man's switch (the letter)
- The backup records he doesn't know exist
- The party's resourcefulness

If the party survives the first 72 hours, Silvermark will be forced into increasingly desperate actions, creating opportunities to expose him.

### If the Party Gets Stuck

**Hints to Deploy:**
1. Captain Marris mentions evidence room irregularities
2. NPC at Brass Scales mentions Aldric "waiting to deliver something important"
3. Vex's signet ring note discovered during inventory review
4. Marion Thresh's body surfaces, confirming pattern of poisoning

**Deus Ex Machina (Last Resort):**
- Anonymous tip delivered to party (from Aldric Grenn or unknown benefactor)
- Captain Marris discovers evidence tampering independently
- Silvermark makes public mistake due to overconfidence

Avoid using these unless party is genuinely stuck—the satisfaction comes from players solving it themselves.
