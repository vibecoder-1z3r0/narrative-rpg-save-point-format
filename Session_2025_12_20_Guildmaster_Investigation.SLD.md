## Opening Scene

Rain hammers the cobblestones of the Merchants' Quarter as Kael and Sera approach the Guildmaster's Tower. The quarterly auction ended an hour ago, and the building's usual bustle has given way to eerie quiet. Through the tower's high windows, lamplight flickers—Vex is still in his office, alone with his ledgers.

The party has spent three sessions tracking the smuggling operation. Tonight, they have enough evidence to confront the Guildmaster directly. Sera has already scouted the building's three entrances. Kael carries forged documents that will get them past the ground floor guards—if anyone asks.

The tower looms ahead, and somewhere inside, Guildmaster Vex is waiting.

---

## Major Events

- **19:47 - Infiltration**: Sera picked the tower's eastern service entrance using her Whisper Tools. Stealth check: 17 vs DC 14. Success. No alarm triggered.

- **19:52 - Evidence Discovery**: Kael located the smuggling ledger in Vex's desk (third drawer, false bottom). Investigation check: 19 vs DC 16. Success. Ledger contains three months of coded shipping manifests referencing "The Architect."

- **20:05 - Confrontation Begins**: Party confronted Vex in his office. Vex initially cooperative, then defensive when presented with ledger evidence. Persuasion check (Kael): 12 vs DC 15. Failure. Vex refused to cooperate.

- **20:18 - Escalation**: Vex threatened to call city watch and have the party arrested for breaking and entering. Intimidation check (Sera): 16 vs DC 14. Success. Vex admitted knowledge of smuggling but claimed he was "following orders."

- **20:23 - Sudden Death**: Mid-sentence, Vex clutched his chest, gasped, and collapsed. Medicine check (Kael): 18. Diagnosis: Poison, fast-acting, administered within the last hour. Vex dead before intervention possible.

- **20:25 - Watch Arrival**: City watch burst through the office door (suspiciously fast response time). Led by Captain Marris. Party found standing over Vex's body with evidence in hand.

- **20:30 - Accusations**: Captain Marris placed both party members under provisional arrest pending investigation. Evidence seized. Torven Brasscoin arrived outside tower, making statements to gathered crowd about "assassins."

- **20:45 - Separation**: Party taken to separate interrogation rooms at watch headquarters.

## Important Interactions

**Kael and Guildmaster Vex:**

Kael presented the ledger as evidence of smuggling.

Vex: "Where did you get that? That's private guild property!"

Kael: "These manifests show stolen relics moving through your auctions. You're profiting from theft."

Vex (visibly shaken): "You don't understand what you've stumbled into. The Architect doesn't forgive interference."

Kael: "Then help us stop them. Testify."

Vex: "You're a fool. The Architect owns half the council. Testifying is suicide."

[Vex begins reaching for a bell pull to summon guards, then suddenly gasps and collapses]

---

**Captain Marris and Kael:**

Marris (conflicted): "Kael, what in the hells happened here?"

Kael: "We found evidence of smuggling. Vex was poisoned—we didn't do this."

Marris: "You're found standing over a dead guildmaster with a stolen ledger in your hands. What am I supposed to think?"

Kael: "Check the body. Fast-acting poison, administered before we arrived. Someone knew we'd be here."

Marris (quietly): "That may be true. But I have to take you in. Protocol."

---

**Torven Brasscoin (to the crowd):**

"Guildmaster Vex, a pillar of our community, murdered in cold blood by hired killers! These so-called 'investigators' broke into his office under cover of darkness. The city watch found them standing over his body! I call for immediate justice!"

## Session Narrative

What began as a straightforward confrontation spiraled into catastrophe within minutes.

The party's careful preparation—three sessions of investigation, forged documents, scouted routes—meant nothing once Vex collapsed. The evidence they risked everything to obtain was confiscated. Their reputations were destroyed in a single public accusation from Torven Brasscoin.

Most troubling: someone else wanted Vex dead and timed the poisoning to coincide with the party's infiltration. The watch arrived suspiciously fast, as if pre-positioned. And Vex's final words about "The Architect" suggest the conspiracy runs deeper than a single corrupt guildmaster.

The session ended with the party separated, under arrest, and unable to pursue the investigation. The next session will begin with interrogation scenes and a critical decision: do they cooperate with the watch and risk losing the evidence trail, or do they break out and become fugitives?

## Legendary Acknowledgments

As Guildmaster Vex's body was carried from the tower, old merchants in the crowd made signs against evil and whispered of the Broken Scales—an ancient curse said to fall upon those who betray the guild's founding principles.

Whether superstition or something darker, the rain intensified as the body passed, and three gas lamps along the street flickered and died in sequence.

Dawn breaks over Ironhaven. The Merchants' Quarter remains cordoned off by city watch, yellow banners marking the crime scene. Kael sits alone in an interrogation room, staring at the ceiling, running through every detail of the confrontation.

In an identical room two floors above, Sera is already planning escape routes.

And across the quarter, in the Brass Scales Inn, Torven Brasscoin raises a glass with three council members, toasting "a new era of guild leadership."
