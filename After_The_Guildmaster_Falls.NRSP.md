## Narrative Context

### Summary

The party's investigation into merchant guild corruption reached its breaking point. After infiltrating the quarterly auction and discovering evidence of smuggling operations, they confronted Guildmaster Vex in his private office. The confrontation ended in disaster—Vex collapsed during the argument, dead before the city watch arrived.

The evidence the party gathered proves Vex was moving stolen relics through legitimate trade routes, but their presence at the scene has made them prime suspects in his death. The Merchants' Quarter is now locked down, and three rival guild members are already maneuvering to claim Vex's position.

### Unresolved Threads

- The poison that killed Vex was not carried by the party—someone else wanted him dead
- Vex's ledger contains coded references to "The Architect" who orchestrated the smuggling network
- The city watch is holding the auction house sealed pending investigation
- Rival guildmaster Torven Brasscoin is spreading rumors that the party are assassins-for-hire
- The stolen relics have already left Ironhaven—their destination unknown

## Session Log

### Opening Scene

Rain hammers the cobblestones of the Merchants' Quarter as Kael and Sera approach the Guildmaster's Tower. The quarterly auction ended an hour ago, and the building's usual bustle has given way to eerie quiet. Through the tower's high windows, lamplight flickers—Vex is still in his office, alone with his ledgers.

This is the moment they've been working toward for three sessions. The evidence is solid. All they need is a confession.

### Major Events

- **Infiltration Success**: Sera picked the tower's side entrance; no alarms triggered
- **Evidence Discovery**: Kael found the smuggling ledger in Vex's desk drawer
- **Confrontation**: Vex denied involvement, grew increasingly agitated
- **Sudden Death**: Vex collapsed mid-argument, clutching his chest
- **Watch Arrival**: City watch burst in moments later, weapons drawn
- **Accusations**: The party became prime suspects despite protests
- **Evidence Seized**: Watch confiscated the ledger and sealed the auction house

### Important Interactions

- Guildmaster Vex accused the party of fabricating evidence and threatened legal action
- Captain Marris (Kael's former commander) arrived with the watch, visibly conflicted
- Torven Brasscoin appeared outside the tower, making statements to gathered merchants about "hired killers"

### Closing Image

As dawn breaks over Ironhaven, the Merchants' Quarter remains cordoned off by city watch. Kael and Sera sit in separate interrogation rooms, separated for the first time since the investigation began. Through barred windows, they can see Torven Brasscoin shaking hands with council members on the steps of the Brass Scales Inn.

The evidence they risked everything to obtain is locked in a watch evidence room. Vex is dead. And someone else knew they would be in that tower tonight.
