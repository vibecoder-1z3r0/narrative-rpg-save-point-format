## Backstory

Silas Coalhand was born in the Underdocks—the cramped, smoke-filled neighborhoods beneath Coalport's main platforms where dock workers, coal haulers, and airship maintenance crews lived in tenements that never saw sunlight.

His father died in a boiler explosion when Silas was eight. His mother worked three jobs to keep the family housed and fed. Silas learned early that the world was divided into those who gave orders and those who followed them—and that crossing that line required more than competence. It required connections, money, and luck.

At sixteen, Silas enlisted in the Airship Security Corps, a military organization responsible for protecting commercial dirigibles from pirates, saboteurs, and unrest. He served under Captain Ironwood aboard the *Iron Duke*, where he met Marcus Wrenchwright—Adelaide's father—and learned that some officers actually cared about their crew.

Silas distinguished himself during the Borderlands Conflict, where he prevented a sabotage attempt that would have destroyed the *Iron Duke* and its 200-person crew. Ironwood recommended him for commendation, but military politics buried the recognition. Working-class heroes made officers uncomfortable.

After twelve years of service, Silas retired to private investigation work in Coalport. He specialized in airship security consultations and industrial sabotage cases—combining his military training with hard-won street wisdom.

When Adelaide needed a partner for dangerous investigative work, she recruited Silas. They've worked together for three years, solving cases that blend engineering mysteries with human treachery.

The invitation to join the *Brass Phoenix*'s maiden voyage was supposed to be a celebration—a chance to reconnect with Captain Ironwood and see Adelaide recognized for her brilliance.

Instead, it became another sabotage case. Some things never change.

### Key Abilities

**Combat Veteran** (Passive)
Silas has advantage on initiative rolls when aboard airships or in enclosed industrial spaces. His military training activates instinctively in familiar environments.

**Protect Partner** (Reaction)
When Adelaide is targeted by an attack within 10 feet, Silas can use his reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll. He's spent three years learning to anticipate danger to her.

**Interrogation Pressure** (1/short rest)
Silas can focus intense interrogation on one target for 1 minute. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 15) or reveal one truthful piece of information they're trying to hide.

**Tactical Positioning** (Bonus Action)
Silas can use his bonus action to move up to half his speed without provoking opportunity attacks, then grant an ally within 30 feet advantage on their next attack roll.

**Quick Draw** (Passive)
Silas can draw and fire his revolver as a single action. He's never been caught unarmed when it matters.

## Character Notes

Silas carries the weight of class consciousness like others carry pocket watches. Every interaction with nobility reminds him that competence doesn't erase the Underdocks accent in his voice or the coal dust he can never quite wash from beneath his fingernails.

His partnership with Adelaide works because they're both outsiders trying to prove themselves in systems designed to exclude them. They understand each other's frustrations without needing to explain them.

His feelings for Adelaide complicate everything. He values their professional partnership too much to risk it with a confession that might be unwelcome. So he watches her work with quiet admiration and tells himself that partnership is enough.

The chronometer crisis is testing his ability to work with Captain Ironwood again. Ironwood gave Silas opportunities, but Ironwood is also nobility—someone who can walk away from the consequences that working-class people face. Silas respects the man but doesn't fully trust the system he represents.

If the saboteur turns out to be nobility using working-class people like Graylock as disposable pawns, Silas won't be surprised. He's seen that pattern his entire life.

But this time, he has the authority to do something about it. Captain Ironwood gave him that. And Silas intends to use it.

The *Brass Phoenix* will survive. The saboteur will face justice. And maybe—just maybe—Adelaide will finally see him as something more than a partner.

The chronometer is ticking again. The storm is coming. And Silas Coalhand has work to do.
