## Narrative Context

### Summary

The luxury dirigible *Brass Phoenix* is six hours from Meridian Station when Chief Navigator Castellan discovers the ship's primary chronometer has been sabotaged. Without accurate timekeeping, navigation through the approaching Titanstorm is impossible—the ship will be torn apart by atmospheric shear forces.

The party—traveling as passengers on the Phoenix's maiden voyage—has been recruited by Captain Ironwood to investigate. The sabotage occurred sometime in the last 12 hours, during which only a handful of people had access to the navigation deck. The suspects include crew members, wealthy passengers, and a mysterious inventor who boarded at the last minute.

Evidence suggests this is no random vandalism. The chronometer's cascade gears were deliberately jammed with a custom-machined brass shim that could only have been created in the ship's own machine shop. Someone aboard the Phoenix wants this airship to fail—and they may not be finished.

The party has four hours until the storm boundary. The chronometer can be repaired, but only if they identify the saboteur and recover the missing regulator crystal that was also stolen.

### Unresolved Threads

- The saboteur's motive remains unclear (political? personal vendetta? industrial espionage?)
- Chief Engineer Graylock reported seeing "someone in a leather apron" near the machine shop at 0300 hours
- The missing regulator crystal is not in the navigation deck—someone has hidden it elsewhere on the ship
- Passenger Cordelia Vex has been behaving suspiciously, spending excessive time near restricted areas
- The storm is intensifying faster than predicted—the 4-hour window may shrink
- Someone sent an encrypted aethergram message from the communications deck last night

## Session Log

### Opening Scene

The dining saloon of the *Brass Phoenix* is a marvel of polished brass and rich mahogany. Crystal chandeliers sway gently with the airship's motion as passengers enjoy afternoon tea at 8,000 feet. Through the observation windows, clouds drift past like cotton islands in an endless sky.

The serenity shatters when Captain Ironwood's voice crackles through the speaking tubes: "Adelaide Wrenchwright and Silas Coalhand to the bridge. Immediately."

By the time the party reaches the navigation deck, the situation is clear. Chief Navigator Castellan stands over the opened chronometer housing, his face pale. Inside, brass gears sit frozen in place, jammed by a precision-machined shim wedged into the cascade mechanism.

"Four hours until storm boundary," Castellan says quietly. "Without the chronometer, we fly blind. And flying blind through a Titanstorm means we don't fly at all."

Captain Ironwood turns to the party. "You're investigators. Investigate. Find whoever did this, recover the regulator crystal they stole, and do it before we're all scattered across three counties."

### Major Events

- **Sabotage Discovery**: Chronometer jammed with custom brass shim, regulator crystal missing
- **Machine Shop Investigation**: Adelaide found brass shavings matching the shim's composition
- **Suspect Interviews**: Silas questioned crew and passengers, identified three prime suspects
- **Aethergram Discovery**: Encrypted message sent from communications deck at 0200 hours
- **Hidden Cache Found**: Regulator crystal discovered in cargo hold ventilation shaft
- **Confession Extract**: Under pressure, Chief Engineer Graylock admitted to creating the shim but claims he was blackmailed
- **Storm Acceleration**: Weather officer reports Titanstorm intensifying—window now 3 hours

### Important Interactions

- Captain Ironwood granted the party authority to question anyone and access restricted areas
- Cordelia Vex became defensive when asked about her movements, claimed "passenger privacy"
- Chief Engineer Graylock broke down under interrogation, revealed someone threatened to expose his gambling debts unless he sabotaged navigation
- Anonymous threatening note found in Graylock's quarters: "Do as instructed or your family learns the truth"

### Closing Image

As storm clouds gather on the horizon, Adelaide works feverishly in the navigation deck, reinstalling the recovered regulator crystal. The chronometer's gears begin to turn again, ticking with mechanical precision.

But the mystery isn't solved. Graylock was coerced—someone else orchestrated this sabotage. And with three hours until storm boundary, that someone is still aboard the *Brass Phoenix*, watching, waiting.

Outside the observation windows, lightning flickers in the distance. The Titanstorm is coming.
