## Opening Scene

The dining saloon of the *Brass Phoenix* is a testament to industrial luxury. Polished brass railings gleam under crystal chandeliers that sway with the airship's gentle motion. Passengers in fine clothes—merchants, inventors, minor nobility—enjoy afternoon tea while clouds drift past the observation windows like silent witnesses to their elevation.

Adelaide Wrenchwright adjusts her brass goggles and studies a navigation chart spread across her table. Silas Coalhand sits across from her, methodically cleaning his service revolver—a habit from his years in airship security.

The speaking tube above their table hisses, then Captain Ironwood's voice crackles through: "Adelaide Wrenchwright and Silas Coalhand to the bridge. Immediately. This is not a request."

The urgency in his tone brings both to their feet. As they climb the brass spiral staircase to the navigation deck, the ship's chronometer chimes the hour—a sound that will soon become ominously absent.

---

## Major Events

### 14:15 - Discovery of Sabotage

Captain Ironwood and Chief Navigator Castellan stand over the opened chronometer housing. Adelaide immediately recognizes the problem—a brass shim jammed into the cascade gears, preventing the mechanism from advancing.

**Adelaide (Investigation check: 21 vs DC 16):** "This wasn't accidental. The shim is precision-machined, probably created in our own machine shop. And the regulator crystal is missing—someone removed it deliberately."

**Captain Ironwood:** "We're six hours from Meridian Station with a Titanstorm forming dead ahead. Without the chronometer, we cannot navigate safely. You have four hours before I must decide whether to attempt storm passage or risk an emergency landing in hostile territory."

---

### 14:30 - Machine Shop Investigation

Adelaide and Silas descend to the engineering deck. The machine shop is a symphony of rotating belts, steam pistons, and the smell of hot metal.

**Adelaide (Investigation check: 19):** She discovers brass shavings on the floor near the lathe, matching the composition of the sabotage shim. The lathe's operation log shows it was used at 02:47 this morning—during the night watch when most of the ship slept.

**Chief Engineer Graylock (appearing in doorway):** "What are you two doing in my shop?"

**Silas (Insight check: 17):** Graylock's hands are trembling. His eyes dart to the lathe, then away. He's nervous about something.

---

### 15:00 - Passenger Interviews

The party questions passengers in the saloon. Most have alibis—they were asleep or can account for their movements. But three stand out:

**Cordelia Vex** (wealthy merchant, traveling alone):
- Nervous and defensive when questioned
- Claims she was in her cabin all night, but cabin steward didn't see her return until 04:00
- Carries a locked brass case she refuses to open
- **Persuasion check (Silas): 12 vs DC 15. Failure.** She refuses to cooperate further.

**Lord Ashworth** (minor nobility, gambling debts):
- Admits he was awake gambling in the cargo hold until 03:30
- Witnessed Chief Engineer Graylock heading toward the navigation deck around 03:00
- **Insight check (Adelaide): 16.** He's telling the truth but hiding something about his gambling partners.

**Professor Minerva Cogsworth** (inventor, boarded last minute):
- Claims she was working in her cabin on "mechanical theories"
- Her cabin is directly below the navigation deck
- **Investigation check (Adelaide): 18.** Adelaide notices brass dust on Professor's sleeves—similar to machine shop residue.

---

### 15:45 - Communications Deck Discovery

Silas checks the aethergram log. An encrypted message was transmitted at 02:17 hours to an address in the Free Territories—known for industrial espionage operations.

**Silas (Investigation check: 20):** The transmission was sent from the emergency override panel, bypassing normal logging. Only someone with engineering knowledge could do this.

---

### 16:20 - Confronting Chief Engineer Graylock

Adelaide and Silas corner Graylock in the engine room. Under pressure, he breaks.

**Graylock (voice shaking):** "I didn't want to! They said they'd expose my gambling debts to the crew if I didn't help them. My family would be ruined!"

**Intimidation check (Silas): 18 vs DC 14. Success.**

Graylock admits he created the brass shim and helped jam the chronometer—but he was blackmailed. Someone aboard the ship knew about his debts and threatened to expose him unless he sabotaged the navigation systems.

**Graylock:** "They left me a note in my quarters. No signature. Just instructions and threats. I was supposed to create the shim and leave the regulator crystal in the cargo hold ventilation shaft. I never saw who picked it up."

---

### 16:45 - Cargo Hold Search

The party descends to the cargo hold—a cavernous space filled with crates, luggage, and the mysterious "special shipment" marked with Meridian Station seals.

**Adelaide (Investigation check: 22):** She locates the ventilation shaft. Inside, wrapped in oilcloth, is the missing regulator crystal—still intact.

**Silas (Perception check: 19):** He spots fresh scuff marks on the crate containing the "special shipment." Someone has been trying to access it.

---

### 17:10 - Storm Warning

Weather Officer Hastings bursts onto the navigation deck: "Captain! Titanstorm is intensifying faster than models predicted. We now have three hours, not four. If we don't have navigation online soon, we'll have to abort to emergency landing."

Pressure mounts. The party has the regulator crystal, but they still don't know who orchestrated the sabotage or why.

---

### 17:30 - Chronometer Repair

Adelaide works with Navigator Castellan to reinstall the regulator crystal and remove the brass shim. Her mechanist expertise is critical.

**Mechanist check (Adelaide): 23 vs DC 18. Success.**

The chronometer's gears begin turning again. Tick. Tick. Tick. The sound of precision machinery restored.

**Navigator Castellan:** "We're back online. I can navigate the storm now."

But Captain Ironwood's expression remains grim. The saboteur is still aboard, and their motive remains unknown.

## Important Interactions

**Captain Ironwood and Adelaide:**

Ironwood: "I served with your father on the *Iron Duke*. He taught me that every mechanical failure tells a story. What's this one saying?"

Adelaide: "This wasn't about destroying the ship. The sabotage was precise—delaying us, not killing us. Someone wants the Phoenix to miss our arrival window at Meridian Station."

Ironwood: "The trade summit. If we're late, the Consolidated Steamworks contract goes to our competitors."

---

**Silas and Lord Ashworth (in the cargo hold):**

Silas: "You said you witnessed Graylock near navigation. What were you really doing awake at that hour?"

Ashworth (reluctantly): "I was gambling. With Cordelia Vex. She's not a merchant—she's a professional card sharp working for the Free Territories. She's been trying to access the special shipment since we left port."

Silas: "And you didn't report this because?"

Ashworth: "Because I owe her 3,000 crowns and she promised to forgive the debt if I stayed silent."

---

**Adelaide and Professor Cogsworth:**

Adelaide: "You have machine shop residue on your sleeves. Care to explain?"

Cogsworth: "I visited the shop yesterday afternoon to requisition materials for my research. Perfectly legitimate."

Adelaide (Insight: 20): "You're lying. That's fresh brass dust—from last night. You were in the shop after the sabotage."

Cogsworth (stammering): "I... I heard someone working the lathe at an unusual hour. I went to investigate but they'd already left. I didn't think it mattered!"

## Session Narrative

What began as a relaxing voyage aboard the *Brass Phoenix*'s maiden journey became a race against time and atmospheric fury. The sabotage of the chronometer wasn't random vandalism—it was a calculated act of industrial espionage designed to delay the airship's arrival at Meridian Station.

The party's investigation revealed a web of coercion, blackmail, and hidden motives. Chief Engineer Graylock, crushed by gambling debts, was forced to create the sabotage mechanism. Cordelia Vex, posing as a wealthy merchant, is actually an agent working for competitors in the Free Territories. And Professor Cogsworth, despite her claims of innocence, witnessed the sabotage but said nothing—possibly complicit, possibly terrified.

The chronometer has been restored. The *Brass Phoenix* can now navigate the Titanstorm. But the orchestrator of the sabotage remains free aboard the ship, and the "special shipment" in the cargo hold may be the real target of this conspiracy.

As storm clouds gather and lightning flickers on the horizon, the party must decide: continue the investigation and risk missing the narrow navigation window, or focus on surviving the storm and deal with the saboteur afterward.

## Legendary Acknowledgments

As the chronometer resumed its precise ticking, old sailors in the engine room made the sign of the Brass Cog—an ancient gesture meant to ward off mechanical failure.

For a moment, the ship's pressure gauges all aligned perfectly, their needles pointing to optimal readings simultaneously—a phenomenon engineers call "the Harmonious Second." Some say it's luck. Others say it's the ship itself, grateful to be whole again.

## Closing Image

The navigation deck glows amber in the light of setting sun filtering through storm clouds. Adelaide closes the chronometer housing, its gears ticking with renewed precision. Silas stands at the observation window, watching lightning dance through distant thunderheads.

Captain Ironwood plots a course through the storm's eye, his face illuminated by the soft glow of pressure lamps and navigation charts.

Somewhere below, in passenger quarters or crew cabins, the saboteur waits. The chronometer is fixed, but the conspiracy is far from over.

And ahead, the Titanstorm churns the sky into chaos—a wall of wind and lightning that will test every rivet, every seam, every soul aboard the *Brass Phoenix*.

The ship's whistle sounds three times: the storm approach signal.

The real test begins now.

**[16:20] - Graylock Breaks**

---

**[17:30] - Chronometer Repair**

Adelaide (Player): I've got the regulator crystal back. I want to repair the chronometer.

GM: This is a difficult task—the cascade gears are delicate and improperly aligned. Give me a Mechanist check, DC 18.

Adelaide (Player): I use my Precision Toolkit for advantage. First roll... 23!

GM: Your hands move with the precision of the mechanism itself. You remove the brass shim, realign the cascade gears, and carefully reinstall the regulator crystal. The chronometer's balance wheel begins to oscillate. The gears engage. Tick. Tick. Tick. Navigation is restored.

Navigator Castellan: "By the Brass Cog, you've done it. We can navigate the storm now."

Captain Ironwood: "Excellent work. But we still have a saboteur aboard, and they may not be finished."
