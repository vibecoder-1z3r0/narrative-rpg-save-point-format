# The Chronometer Crisis

**Genre:** Steampunk Mystery / Industrial Espionage
**System:** Clockwork-and-Chimneys (adaptable to any steampunk system)
**Campaign Arc:** BP-03

## Overview

A complete steampunk campaign session demonstrating all 6 NRSP file types in an airship sabotage mystery.

The luxury dirigible *Brass Phoenix* is on her maiden voyage when her primary chronometer is sabotaged. Without accurate timekeeping, navigating through the approaching Titanstorm is impossible—and the storm window is closing. The party must identify the saboteur, repair the damage, and uncover a conspiracy of industrial espionage—all within hours of the storm boundary.

## The Conspiracy

The chronometer sabotage isn't random vandalism—it's industrial espionage designed to:
1. Delay the *Brass Phoenix* past trade summit deadline
2. Cause Meridian Airship Lines to forfeit a 2-million crown contract
3. Enable theft of prototype engine technology worth 100,000 crowns
4. Give Free Territories competitors market dominance

Chief Engineer Graylock was blackmailed into creating the sabotage mechanism, but the true orchestrators are Cordelia Vex (industrial spy) and Professor Cogsworth (technical specialist).

## Pressure Points

**Storm Deadline:** 4 hours → 3 hours → 2 hours (actual) → navigate or abort decision

**Escalating Sabotage:** If Cordelia remains free, she'll sabotage backup chronometer

**Explosive Threat:** If cornered, Professor Cogsworth threatens cargo hold detonation

**Class Tensions:** Crew vs. passengers, Adelaide's gender barriers, Silas's outsider status

## Success Conditions

**Complete Victory:**
- Identify both conspirators
- Navigate storm successfully
- Arrive on time for trade summit
- Protect prototype engine cargo

**Partial Success:**
- Navigate storm but saboteurs escape
- OR catch saboteurs but miss deadline
- OR survive with casualties

**Failure:**
- Ship destroyed in storm
- Saboteurs steal prototypes
- Party blamed for disaster

## GM Notes

**Pacing:** The 4-hour deadline creates natural time pressure. Reduce available time each hour to increase tension.

**NPC Motivations:**
- Graylock: Redemption through cooperation
- Cordelia: Mission success through deception
- Cogsworth: Mission success without mass murder
- Ashworth: Survival through self-interest

**Moral Complexity:** Graylock is both victim and perpetrator. Cogsworth is brilliant but compromised. The party must decide who deserves punishment vs. mercy.

**Class Themes:** The working-class crew (Adelaide, Silas, Graylock) are trapped by upper-class manipulation (Cordelia, nobles, industrial competition). Justice should acknowledge power imbalances.
