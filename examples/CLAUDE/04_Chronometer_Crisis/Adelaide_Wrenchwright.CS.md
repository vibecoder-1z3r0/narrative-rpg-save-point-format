## Backstory

Adelaide Wrenchwright grew up in the industrial district of Coalport, where the sky is perpetually gray with coal smoke and the streets echo with the rhythm of steam hammers. Her father, Marcus Wrenchwright, was chief engineer aboard the military dirigible *Iron Duke* before retiring with honors—and chronic lung damage from years breathing recycled air.

From age six, Adelaide spent her days in her father's workshop, learning to read mechanical systems the way other children learn to read books. By twelve, she could rebuild a steam engine from memory. By sixteen, she had designed improvements to chronometer mechanisms that her father quietly passed to colleagues in the Navy.

She never attended the Royal Engineering Academy—her family couldn't afford the tuition, and the Academy didn't accept women regardless. Instead, she apprenticed under a series of master mechanists, each one grudgingly admitting she was the most talented student they'd ever trained.

Adelaide carries a chip on her shoulder about class and credentials. She's better than most Academy-trained engineers and knows it, but society refuses to acknowledge her brilliance because she lacks the right pedigree and gender.

When Captain Ironwood—her father's former commanding officer—invited her aboard the *Brass Phoenix*'s maiden voyage as a consulting mechanist, she saw an opportunity to prove herself on the grandest stage: the luxury airship market.

Then someone sabotaged the chronometer, and Adelaide's voyage became a test of everything her father ever taught her.

## Combat Details

### Combat Role
Adelaide avoids combat when possible, preferring to solve problems with engineering rather than violence. When forced to fight, she uses improvised weapons and environmental advantages.

### Tactics
- Use workshop tools as improvised weapons (wrenches, hammers)
- Target mechanical weak points on steam-powered enemies
- Disable machinery to create hazards for enemies
- Coordinate with Silas for tactical positioning
- Retreat to defensible positions with cover

### Key Abilities

**Mechanical Insight** (1/short rest)
As a bonus action, Adelaide can analyze a mechanical system (including steam-powered constructs or traps) and learn: current condition, weak points, or how to disable it.

**Emergency Repair** (3/long rest)
Adelaide can perform rapid field repairs on damaged equipment, restoring functionality for 1 hour. This includes weapons, armor, or environmental systems.

**Precision Strike** (Passive)
When Adelaide attacks with improvised mechanical weapons (wrenches, hammers, tools), she may use INT modifier instead of STR for attack and damage rolls.

**System Override** (1/long rest)
Adelaide can hack or override a mechanical or steam-powered system within 30 feet. This might disable a security lock, stop an engine, or trigger emergency protocols.

## Character Notes

Adelaide is driven by a need to prove herself in a world that systematically undervalues her. Every engineering problem is a test—not just of her skills, but of whether society will ever recognize her brilliance without the stamp of institutional approval.

The chronometer crisis is both her worst nightmare (failure could ruin her reputation) and her greatest opportunity (success proves she's better than credentialed Academy engineers).

Her partnership with Silas is complicated. He treats her as an equal and values her expertise, which is rare enough that she treasures the relationship. But his obvious romantic interest creates tension she doesn't know how to navigate—her life has been machines and precision, not emotions and uncertainty.

If the *Brass Phoenix* survives this crisis intact, Adelaide's career will be made. If it fails, she'll be blamed for not preventing the sabotage—regardless of whether she bears any responsibility.

The chronometer is ticking again. But the pressure remains.
