## Secret Motives

Chief Engineer Magnus Graylock never wanted to sabotage the *Brass Phoenix*. He loves this ship—spent two years overseeing her construction, personally calibrated every steam valve, tested every pressure seal.

But six months ago, his gambling habit caught up with him. What started as casual card games in Coalport taverns escalated into high-stakes poker with dangerous people. By the time he realized the games were rigged, he owed 8,000 crowns to the Underdock Syndicate—more than three years' salary.

The Syndicate gave him two options: pay immediately, or they'd inform his wife and the Meridian Airship Lines board of directors about his debts. Either way, his career and marriage would be destroyed.

Then, three days before the *Brass Phoenix*'s maiden voyage, he found a sealed letter in his cabin aboard the ship:

> "Your debts can disappear. Follow these instructions exactly: Create a brass shim to the enclosed specifications. Use it to jam the chronometer's cascade gears on the night of the 13th. Remove the regulator crystal and place it in cargo hold ventilation shaft C-7. Do this, and your debt will be erased. Refuse, and your family learns everything."

The letter included detailed chronometer schematics that Graylock had never shown anyone—proof that whoever sent this had access to confidential engineering documents.

Graylock agonized for two days. Then, terrified for his family and his career, he did exactly as instructed.

He created the shim at 02:47 hours on the 13th, using the machine shop lathe when the ship was mostly asleep. At 03:15, he accessed the navigation deck using his chief engineer override codes. He jammed the chronometer and removed the regulator crystal, leaving it in the specified ventilation shaft.

He hasn't slept since. Every time the ship's whistles sound or someone knocks on his cabin door, he expects to be arrested.

When Adelaide and Silas confronted him in the engine room, part of him was relieved. The secret was destroying him.

## Secret Inventory

### Hidden in Graylock's Cabin

- **Blackmail Letter** (under mattress)
  The original threatening letter with chronometer specifications. Written on expensive paper, no signature. Handwriting analysis would reveal it was written by someone with engineering training—consistent letter spacing, technical diagrams precisely drawn.

- **Gambling Debt Records** (locked drawer)
  Ledger documenting six months of escalating poker losses. Final entry: "8,000 crowns owed to V.C." (the initials V.C. match Cordelia Vex's cover identity, though Graylock doesn't know her real name).

- **Engineering Schematic** (included with blackmail letter)
  Detailed drawings of the *Brass Phoenix* chronometer showing the exact location to insert the jamming shim. These specifications are confidential—only three people should have access: Graylock, the head chronometer manufacturer, and Captain Ironwood.

- **Empty Whiskey Bottle** (trash bin)
  Evidence of Graylock's deteriorating state. He normally doesn't drink on duty.

### On Graylock's Person

- **Chief Engineer Badge**
  Official credentials granting access to all engineering systems, including navigation deck override.

- **Pocket Watch** (Malfunctioning)
  Ironic: the chronometer saboteur's own timepiece runs five minutes fast. Graylock hasn't noticed because he's been too stressed to care.

- **Wedding Ring** (Gold)
  Engraved: "To Magnus, my anchor—L.G." (his wife, Lydia). Graylock keeps touching it unconsciously, a nervous habit that developed after receiving the blackmail letter.

## Secret Relationships

**Cordelia Vex** (Unknown Blackmailer)
- Graylock doesn't know Cordelia orchestrated his blackmail
- He's only seen her as a passenger in the saloon
- Cordelia ran the rigged poker games that created his debts, then used those debts to coerce him
- She needed someone with navigation deck access to execute the chronometer sabotage

**The Underdock Syndicate** (Creditors)
- Criminal organization that controls illegal gambling in Coalport
- Graylock owes them 8,000 crowns
- The Syndicate is working with Cordelia Vex (though Graylock doesn't know this)
- If the sabotage is exposed, the Syndicate will still demand payment—Graylock's cooperation doesn't erase the debt

**Lydia Graylock** (Wife, Unaware)
- Lydia doesn't know about her husband's gambling addiction or debts
- She's proud of Magnus for being chief engineer on the *Brass Phoenix*
- Graylock is terrified she'll discover his betrayal
- His primary fear isn't arrest—it's losing her respect

**Captain Ironwood** (Betrayed Trust)
- Ironwood personally selected Graylock as chief engineer
- Graylock feels immense guilt for betraying that trust
- Ironwood doesn't yet know about the blackmail circumstances
- When revealed, Ironwood will be more disappointed than angry—which will be worse for Graylock

**Adelaide Wrenchwright** (Complicated)
- Graylock initially resented Adelaide as a woman "supervising" his work
- After his confession, he now sees her as the person who might save the ship he endangered
- Graylock wants to help her repair the chronometer as partial redemption
- He knows his cooperation won't erase his guilt

## GM Notes

### Truth About Graylock's Role

Graylock is a victim turned accomplice. He committed sabotage under duress, which doesn't excuse the act but explains it. He's fundamentally decent—a skilled engineer who made terrible choices when trapped by circumstances.

Cordelia Vex (the real mastermind) deliberately selected Graylock because:
1. He had gambling debts she could exploit
2. He had chief engineer access to navigation systems
3. He's not sophisticated enough to recognize he was being manipulated
4. His guilt would keep him silent even if he wanted to confess

The blackmail letter proves someone had access to confidential chronometer specifications. This narrows suspects to: Captain Ironwood, the manufacturing company, or someone who stole the documents.

Cordelia obtained the specifications through industrial espionage—her employers in the Free Territories want Meridian Airship Lines to fail.

### What Graylock Knows

- **Who threatened him:** Unknown (the letter had no signature)
- **How they knew about his debts:** Unknown (but they knew exact amounts)
- **Why they wanted the chronometer sabotaged:** Unknown (he never questioned it)
- **Whether others are involved:** Unknown (he followed instructions, didn't investigate)
- **Location of regulator crystal:** Cargo hold ventilation shaft C-7 (as instructed)

### What Graylock Doesn't Know

- Cordelia Vex orchestrated everything
- His gambling debts were artificially created through rigged games
- The sabotage is industrial espionage, not random sabotage
- The *Brass Phoenix* carries a "special shipment" that's the real target
- His blackmail letter was written by Professor Cogsworth (who is secretly working for Cordelia)

### Graylock's Future

**If the party exposes him but understands the blackmail context:**
- Captain Ironwood may show mercy and allow him to help repair the chronometer
- Graylock will cooperate fully, desperate for redemption
- His career is finished, but criminal charges might be avoided
- He'll need protection from the Underdock Syndicate (who still want their money)

**If the party treats him as a willing saboteur:**
- Graylock will be arrested and charged with sabotage
- His family will be destroyed by scandal
- The real conspirators (Cordelia, Professor Cogsworth) remain free
- The party misses evidence that could lead to the true mastermind

**If Cordelia learns Graylock confessed:**
- She'll attempt to silence him before he can provide evidence
- "Accident" in the engine room seems likely
- The party will need to protect Graylock to keep their witness alive

### Clues Graylock Can Provide

1. **The blackmail letter** (physical evidence, handwriting sample)
2. **Chronometer specification documents** (proves insider knowledge)
3. **Gambling debt records** (connect to "V.C." initials)
4. **Timeline of the sabotage** (exact times, procedures used)
5. **Regulator crystal location** (cargo hold ventilation shaft C-7)

### Redemption Arc Possibility

If the party chooses mercy over punishment, Graylock can become an ally:
- Uses his engineering knowledge to help identify the true conspirator
- Analyzes the blackmail letter's technical diagrams (recognizes Professor Cogsworth's drafting style)
- Provides access codes and insider knowledge of ship systems
- Helps during storm navigation (his guilt drives him to save the ship he endangered)

But redemption doesn't erase consequences. Even if forgiven by the party, Graylock must live with knowing he almost destroyed the ship he helped build.

### The Real Question

The GM should decide: Is Graylock a villain or a victim?

The answer determines whether the party gets an ally who helps expose Cordelia, or a broken man who goes to prison while the real conspirators escape justice.

The chronometer is ticking again. But Graylock's fate is still being decided.
