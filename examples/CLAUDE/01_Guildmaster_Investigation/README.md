# The Guildmaster Investigation

**Genre:** Fantasy Political Intrigue
**System:** Blades-in-the-Shadow
**Campaign Arc:** IH-04

## Overview

A complete campaign session demonstrating all 6 NRSP file types working together in a political intrigue scenario.

The party's investigation into merchant guild corruption has gone catastrophically wrong. After confronting Guildmaster Vex with evidence of smuggling operations, Vex was poisoned and died in front of them. Now the investigators are prime suspects in his murder, trapped in a conspiracy deeper than they realized.

## Files Included

### Save Point
- **After_The_Guildmaster_Falls.NRSP.md** - Narrative state after Vex's death

### Session Log
- **Session_2025_12_20_Guildmaster_Investigation.SLD.md** - Full play transcript with timestamps, dice rolls, and dialogue

### Character Sheets (Public)
- **Kael_Ironbound.CS.md** - Former city watch investigator (PC)
- **Sera_Nightwhisper.CS.md** - Information broker and infiltrator (PC)

### NPC Sheet (GM-Only)
- **Guildmaster_Vex.NPC.md** - Victim's secrets, hidden motives, and truth about his death

### Location Sheets
- **Merchants_Quarter.LS.md** - Public location information (district overview, points of interest)
- **Merchants_Quarter.LGM.md** - GM-only location secrets (conspiracy details, hidden actors, future triggers)

## What This Demonstrates

✅ **Complete modular system** - All 6 file types linked together
✅ **Public/GM information split** - Players see CS/LS, GM sees NPC/LGM
✅ **State vs History** - NRSP (compressed state) vs SLD (full transcript)
✅ **Cross-file references** - Proper linking between related files
✅ **Timeline tracking** - Previous/Next/Alternate SavePoints
✅ **Practical usability** - A GM could run this session from these files
