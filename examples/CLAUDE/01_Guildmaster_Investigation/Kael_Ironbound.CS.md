## Backstory

Kael Ironbound served as a city watch investigator for twelve years before resigning in protest over corruption within the ranks. When evidence of smuggling operations disappeared from the watch evidence room three times in a single month, Kael realized the rot went deeper than street-level bribery.

He left the watch with his badge, his principles, and a growing list of unanswered questions. Six months later, he began investigating independently, taking on cases the watch "couldn't" solve. His former commander, Captain Marris, warned him to stop digging—advice Kael ignored.

The Guildmaster Vex case was supposed to be straightforward: track the smuggling network, gather evidence, present it to the Council. Instead, it's become a murder investigation with Kael as the prime suspect.

## Character Notes

Kael is driven by a need for justice, but the Vex case has forced him to confront the limits of playing by the rules. His relationship with Sera has introduced him to more flexible methods, and he's slowly accepting that exposing corruption sometimes requires operating outside official channels.

The current situation—arrested for a murder he didn't commit, evidence confiscated, reputation destroyed—is testing whether his principles can survive contact with a rigged system.

If the watch won't clear his name, he'll have to do it himself. And that might mean becoming the fugitive Torven Brasscoin is already claiming he is.
