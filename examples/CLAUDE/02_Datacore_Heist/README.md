# The Datacore Job

**Genre:** Sci-Fi Heist
**System:** Custom (adaptable to Shadowrun, The Sprawl, etc.)
**Campaign Arc:** DC-05

## Overview

A sci-fi heist scenario demonstrating NRSP and SLD files working together.

The crew has infiltrated Nexus Station, a luxury casino and corporate hub orbiting in the Azure Nebula. Their target: a quantum datacore containing evidence of fraud by Helion Industries. The crew has 36 hours before their cover identities expire and they must execute the heist without detection.

## Files Included

### Save Point
- **The_Datacore_Job.NRSP.md** - Team has completed reconnaissance, final prep before the heist

### Session Log

## Key Characters

- **Zephyr** - Con artist and face of the crew
- **Rax** - Muscle and equipment specialist
- **Byte** - Hacker and tech expert
- **Kip** - Inside sympathizer (janitor with maintenance access)

## The Heist

**Objective:** Steal quantum datacore intact and undetected
**Challenges:**
- Biometric locks
- Rotating guard shifts
- AI surveillance
- 36-hour time limit

**Options:**
- Social Engineering Route (forge credentials, talk past security)
- Ventilation Shaft Route (bypass security physically)

## Setting: Nexus Station

A luxury orbital casino and corporate hub featuring:
- High-stakes gambling floors
- Corporate executive suites
- Maintenance corridors (Kip's domain)
- Azure Nebula views
- Heavy security protocols
