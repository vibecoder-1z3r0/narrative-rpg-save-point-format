# The Weeping Spire

**Genre:** Dark Fantasy / Horror
**System:** Generic (adaptable to any dark fantasy system)
**Campaign Arc:** WS-03

## Overview

A dark fantasy scenario demonstrating a standalone NRSP file with branching narrative paths.

The party has reached the abandoned Weeping Spire after three days through the Ashwood. Instead of the sanctuary they hoped for, they've encountered a Weeping—an ancient crystalline entity weeping black ichor that burns the ground. The creature offers them three dangerous paths forward.

✅ **Multiple branching paths** - Three distinct AlternateNext options

## The Three Paths

The Weeping offers the party a choice:

1. **Confront the Oracle** - Climb to the spire's peak, face the Oracle of Lost Names
2. **Flee to the Borderlands** - Escape before the spire fully awakens
3. **Seek the Serpent Cult** - Find those who may know how to seal the breach

Each path leads to different narrative consequences and future savepoints.

## Key Elements

**The Weeping:**
- Ancient crystalline entity
- Weeps black ichor that burns the ground
- Speaks in riddles about "the price of memory" and "the debt the world forgot"
- Neither hostile nor friendly—alien and inscrutable

**The Ashwood:**
- Three days of travel to reach the spire
- Dangerous wilderness

**The Spire:**
- Abandoned tower
- "Alive with something ancient and hungry"
- Contains the Oracle of Lost Names at its peak
- Awakening to unknown purpose
