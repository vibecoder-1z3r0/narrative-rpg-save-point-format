## 🗓 Narrative Context

### Summary
The party has reached the abandoned Weeping Spire after three days of travel through the Ashwood. What they found was not the sanctuary they hoped for—the tower is alive with something ancient and hungry.

A Weeping emerged from the spire's foundation, its crystalline form weeping black ichor that burns the ground. The creature spoke in riddles about "the price of memory" and "the debt the world forgot." It offered the party three paths: confront the Oracle of Lost Names in the tower's peak, flee to the Borderlands before the spire fully awakens, or seek the Serpent Cult who may know how to seal the breach.

The party is split on which path to take. Tensions are high. Resources are low. And the Weeping is still watching.

### Notable Moments
- Kael's sister was mentioned by the Weeping—she disappeared five years ago near this region
- Mira successfully deciphered the Old Script on the spire's entrance: "Memory is the wound; forgetting is the blade"
- Torren's divine magic flickered when the Weeping spoke, suggesting something is interfering with celestial power
- The black ichor burned through Kael's leather boot—he's limping

### Unresolved Threads
- The Oracle of Lost Names—what does it know about the Weepings?
- Kael's sister—is she connected to this place?
- The Serpent Cult's true motives
- Why divine magic is failing near the spire
- The "debt the world forgot"

## 🗺️ Location Snapshots

### Location: The Weeping Spire
- Type: Ancient tower, origin unknown
- Current State: Partially awakened
- Dimensions: ~400 feet tall, 60 feet diameter at base
- Threat Level: Extreme
- Control: The Weeping (entity bound to or emerging from the structure)

#### Notable Features
- Black ichor seeping from foundation cracks (corrosive, magical)
- Old Script inscriptions covering the exterior walls
- Spiral staircase visible through collapsed section of wall
- Faint humming sound emanating from the peak
- No visible entrance at ground level (party found a breach on the eastern wall)

#### Known Levels
| Level            | Status        | Notes                                                    |
|------------------|---------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Foundation       | Breached      | Where the Weeping emerged; ichor pools here              |
| Ground Level     | Collapsed     | Eastern wall has opening large enough for entry          |
| Mid-Levels       | Unknown       | Spiral stairs lead upward; structural integrity unclear  |
| The Oracle's Peak| Sealed (?)    | Mira believes the Oracle of Lost Names resides here      |

#### Hazards
- Corrosive ichor (1d6 acid damage per round of contact)
- Structural instability (risk of collapse)
- Divine magic suppression field (clerics/paladins suffer disadvantage on spell attacks)
- Psychological effect (whispers in Old Script, Wisdom saves to resist fear)
