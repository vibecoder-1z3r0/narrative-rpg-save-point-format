## Backstory

Sera Nightwhisper doesn't talk about her past, and people who ask too many questions tend to find their own secrets suddenly public knowledge.

What is known: She appeared in Ironhaven's Merchants' Quarter five years ago with lockpicking skills, a network of information contacts, and a reputation for getting results. She operates as an independent information broker, selling secrets to the highest bidder—with one exception.

She doesn't work for anyone actively harming the Merchants' Quarter's working-class residents. Call it a principle. Call it a weakness. She calls it a boundary.

When Kael approached her six months ago about auction house theft, she was intrigued. A former city watch investigator, operating independently, actually trying to *help* people? She agreed to one job. Then another. Now they're partners, though neither has defined exactly what that means.

The Vex case was supposed to prove that corruption could be exposed without burning the whole system down. Instead, it's proven that sometimes the system burns you first.

## Combat Details

### Combat Role
Skirmisher and infiltrator. Avoids direct confrontation, strikes from advantage, retreats before retaliation.

### Tactics
- Use Smoke Pellets to create confusion and obscured positions
- Strike from hiding using Sneak Attack
- Target vulnerable enemies first (casters, ranged attackers)
- Retreat and reposition frequently
- Coordinate with Kael for ambush setups

### Key Abilities

**Sneak Attack** (1/turn)
Deal an extra 2d6 damage to one creature Sera hits with an attack if she has advantage on the attack roll, or if an ally is within 5 feet of the target.

**Cunning Action** (Bonus Action)
Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.

**Evasion** (Passive)
When subjected to an effect that allows a Dexterity saving throw for half damage, Sera takes no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.

**Uncanny Dodge** (Reaction)
Halve the damage from one attack that hits Sera, provided she can see the attacker.

## Character Notes

Sera is fundamentally pragmatic, but her partnership with Kael has introduced an unfamiliar variable: she actually cares about the outcome beyond personal survival and profit.

This is inconvenient.

The current situation—arrested, evidence confiscated, escape routes limited—is testing her willingness to prioritize loyalty over self-preservation. She could disappear. The Shadow Broker would provide extraction for the right price. But Kael can't disappear, and leaving him to face this alone feels... wrong.

She hasn't admitted this to Kael. She barely admits it to herself.

If they're going to survive this, she'll need to leverage her information network in ways that risk exposing it. The question is whether clearing their names is worth burning resources she's spent five years building.

The answer should be no.

It isn't.
