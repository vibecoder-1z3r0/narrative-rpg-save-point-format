## Location Overview

The Merchants' Quarter is Ironhaven's commercial heart, a dense urban district where trade, politics, and ambition intersect. Cobblestone streets wind between three-story merchant houses, guild halls, and the towering Guildmaster's Tower that dominates the quarter's eastern edge.

During daylight, the quarter thrums with activity—auction houses conduct quarterly sales, trading companies negotiate shipping contracts, and guild representatives maneuver for political advantage. At night, the district empties quickly as residents retreat behind locked doors, leaving the streets to city watch patrols and those with business best conducted in darkness.

The quarter is currently under lockdown following Guildmaster Vex's death. City watch checkpoints control all major entrances, and residents are questioned when entering or leaving. The atmosphere is tense, with merchants whispering about corruption, assassination, and the power vacuum left by Vex's demise.

## Current State Narrative

The Merchants' Quarter is holding its breath.

Guildmaster Vex's death has thrown the district into political chaos. Three rival candidates—Torven Brasscoin, Merchant-Captain Vellis, and Councilor Thrane Silvermark—are already positioning themselves to claim guild leadership. Each has supporters among the merchant houses, and tensions are rising.

The city watch lockdown has paralyzed commerce. Quarterly auctions have been postponed indefinitely. Trade shipments sit waiting at the docks. Smaller merchants, who operate on thin margins, are beginning to panic as their inventory sits unsold.

Rumors spread through the quarter like wildfire:
- The investigators were hired assassins sent by a rival guild
- Guildmaster Vex was killed for refusing to pay protection money
- The city council ordered Vex's death to cover up corruption
- The lockdown will never lift—Ironhaven is preparing for martial law

The truth is unknown, but fear is abundant.

## Notable Features

### The Guildmaster's Tower

A seven-story stone structure serving as the administrative center of the Merchant Guild. The upper floors contain private offices for guild leadership. The ground floor houses the auction house, where quarterly sales of high-value goods take place.

**Current Status:** Sealed as a crime scene. Watch guards posted at all entrances.

**Architecture:** Old money construction—thick stone walls, narrow windows, a single main staircase flanked by two service staircases. Defensible, but not designed for quick evacuation.

**Security:** Previously moderate (guild guards, locked doors). Now heavy (city watch, posted sentries, restricted access).

---

### The Brass Scales Inn

A neutral-ground establishment where merchants, guild members, and even occasional criminals conduct informal business. The proprietor, a retired merchant named Aldric Grenn, enforces a strict "no violence" policy that all patrons respect.

**Current Status:** Open, but subdued. Serving as an informal gathering point for merchants discussing the succession crisis.

**Reputation:** Trustworthy. Information spoken at The Brass Scales stays at The Brass Scales—unless both parties agree otherwise.

**Notable Services:**
- Private meeting rooms (10 gold/hour)
- Message holding service (for those expecting correspondence)
- Neutral arbitration for merchant disputes

---

### Auction House (Ground Floor of Guildmaster's Tower)

A large open floor space with tiered seating for 200+ attendees. Quarterly auctions sell high-value goods: relics, rare materials, art, magical items, and occasionally "special acquisitions" (items of questionable provenance).

**Current Status:** Sealed. Contains evidence from the quarterly auction held the night of Vex's death.

**Layout:**
- Main auction floor (open space, raised platform)
- Private viewing rooms (for high-value items)
- Basement storage (secure vault for unsold inventory)
- Loading dock (eastern side, for shipment arrivals)

**Reputation:** Legitimate, though rumors of smuggling have circulated for years. No proof until recently.

---

### The Watch Station (Western Gate)

A fortified building serving as the quarter's primary city watch station. Houses 40 watch officers, holding cells for up to 12 prisoners, and an evidence room.

**Current Status:** Operating at maximum capacity due to lockdown operations.

**Command:** Captain Marris (conflicted loyalty, former commander of Kael Ironbound)

**Notable:** The evidence room currently holds Vex's smuggling ledger, confiscated from Kael and Sera.

## Governmental Structure and Hierarchy

### Official Authority

**Merchant Guild Council** (7 members, currently 6 due to Vex's death)
- Governs internal guild affairs, trade regulations, and quarter policies
- Elected by merchant houses (one vote per registered house)
- Current Acting Guildmaster: None (emergency session scheduled in 3 days)

**City Watch** (delegated authority from Ironhaven City Council)
- Maintains order, enforces city law, investigates crimes
- Current Commander for Quarter: Captain Marris
- Current Mission: Investigate Vex's death, maintain lockdown

### Unofficial Power

**The Architect** (identity unknown to public)
- Rumored criminal mastermind controlling smuggling operations
- Suspected of having agents within guild council and city watch
- No confirmed sightings or direct evidence of existence

**Torven Brasscoin** (Candidate for Guildmaster)
- Operates the largest trading company in the quarter
- Publicly campaigns for "reform and transparency"
- Privately consolidating support among merchants who fear investigation

## Location Connections

### Exits and Entrances

- **Northern Gate:** Main entrance, connects to Craftsmen's District (heavy watch presence)
- **Southern Gate:** Connects to docks district (checkpoint, cargo inspections)
- **Eastern Gate:** Connects to residential district (watch checkpoint)
- **Western Gate:** Connects to city center, location of main watch station (heaviest security)

### Secret Passages (Common Knowledge Among Locals)

- Service tunnels beneath auction house (officially for deliveries, unofficially used for smuggling)
- Rooftop paths between merchant houses (used by couriers and thieves)
- Sewer access points (unguarded, but unpleasant and occasionally dangerous)

### Travel Times

- Across quarter (east to west): 20 minutes walking
- To city center: 15 minutes
- To docks: 10 minutes
- To watch station from tower: 5 minutes (explains quick response time)

## Historical Context

The Merchants' Quarter was founded 400 years ago when Ironhaven grew from a fort-town into a trading city. The original merchant houses still stand, their stone foundations predating the current guild system.

The Merchant Guild was established 150 years ago to regulate trade and prevent destructive competition between houses. For most of its history, the guild operated transparently and fairly.

The corruption began approximately 10 years ago, when The Architect (identity unknown) began infiltrating guild operations. By the time anyone noticed the pattern—stolen goods moving through legitimate channels, evidence disappearing, witnesses recanting—the network was too entrenched to root out easily.

Guildmaster Vex's death is the first time the corruption has become publicly undeniable.

## Atmosphere and Culture

**During Business Hours:**
- Busy, professional, competitive
- Merchants negotiate deals over tea and contracts
- Apprentices run between buildings with messages
- Coin changes hands, agreements are sealed

**During Lockdown (Current State):**
- Tense, suspicious, paranoid
- Merchants whisper in corners
- Watch patrols every major street
- Fear of association with Vex or the investigation
- Opportunists positioning for advantage

**Social Norms:**
- Reputation is currency
- Contracts are sacred (breaking one destroys credibility)
- Information is power (gossip travels fast)
- Neutrality is respected (The Brass Scales principle)
- "Business before blood" (but not always)

**Current Mood:** Volatile. The quarter is one rumor away from panic or one arrest away from riots.
