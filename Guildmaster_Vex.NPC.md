## Secret Motives

Guildmaster Vex never wanted to be part of the smuggling operation.

Three years ago, he discovered evidence that "The Architect" had infiltrated the Merchant Guild Council. When Vex attempted to bring the evidence to the city council, he was threatened—not with violence, but with exposure of his own embezzlement from guild funds (a crime he actually committed, years before).

The Architect offered a deal: participate in the smuggling operation as a logistics coordinator, or face public disgrace and imprisonment. Vex chose survival over ethics.

For three years, Vex moved stolen relics through legitimate auction channels, skimming modest profits while The Architect's network grew wealthier. Vex rationalized it as victimless crime—the relics were already stolen, he was merely facilitating their sale.

When Kael and Sera confronted him with evidence, Vex genuinely considered cooperating. His final words—"The Architect owns half the council"—were not a threat. They were a warning.

He died hoping someone would finish what he was too afraid to start.

## Secret Inventory

### Hidden in Vex's Office (Undiscovered)

- **Poison Antidote Vial** (empty)
  Found in Vex's desk drawer, hidden behind a false panel. Vex had been taking regular doses of antidote for weeks, anticipating assassination. The antidote stopped working three days before his death—someone switched it with colored water.

- **Coded Letter from The Architect**
  Sealed envelope addressed to "V" containing instructions for the quarterly auction. The letter includes a cipher key hidden in the wax seal.

- **Embezzlement Records** (Original Evidence)
  Ledger documenting Vex's embezzlement from 12 years ago. The amounts are smaller than expected (~300 gold total), but enough to destroy his reputation.

### On Vex's Person (Confiscated by Watch)

- **Guild Signet Ring**
  Officially just a symbol of office. Actually contains a hidden compartment with a folded note reading: "If you're reading this, I'm dead. Check the auction house basement, third support pillar from the east entrance. -V"

## Secret Relationships

**The Architect** (Unknown Identity)
- Vex never met The Architect face-to-face
- All communications were through coded letters delivered by rotating couriers
- Vex suspected The Architect was a council member but couldn't prove which one
- The Architect knew about Vex's embezzlement before Vex knew he was being watched

**Torven Brasscoin** (Complicated)
- Torven is The Architect's current favored successor for guildmaster
- Torven doesn't know Vex was working for The Architect—he believes Vex was an independent criminal
- Torven's public accusations against Kael and Sera were orchestrated by The Architect to deflect investigation

**Captain Marris** (Regretful)
- Vex and Marris served together in the city watch 20 years ago, before Vex entered politics
- Marris suspected Vex was compromised but had no proof
- Marris's quick arrival at the murder scene was not coincidence—someone tipped him off that "something would happen" at the tower that night

**The Poisoner** (Unknown)
- Vex did not see who poisoned him
- The poison was administered through his nightly tea, prepared by his personal assistant (who disappeared the day before Vex's death)
- The assistant was likely coerced or eliminated after delivering the poisoned tea

## GM Notes

### Truth About Vex's Death

Vex was poisoned by order of The Architect, who determined Vex had become a liability. The timing of the assassination was designed to:
1. Eliminate Vex before he could cooperate with investigators
2. Frame Kael and Sera for murder, discrediting their evidence
3. Create chaos in guild leadership, allowing Torven Brasscoin (The Architect's agent) to seize power

The party was not supposed to escape the tower. The plan was for the city watch to arrest them at the scene, Torven to demand immediate justice, and for both party members to be executed within 48 hours.

Captain Marris's reluctance to treat them as confirmed murderers disrupted this timeline.

### What Vex Knew

- The Architect's identity (he suspected Councilor Thrane Silvermark but wasn't certain)
- The full extent of the smuggling network (15+ merchants across 4 cities)
- The destination of the stolen relics (a private buyer in the Free Cities)
- The location of backup records (hidden in the auction house basement)

### What Vex Didn't Know

- That his personal assistant was working for The Architect
- That his antidote had been replaced with fake liquid
- That Torven Brasscoin was The Architect's agent, not a rival
- That his death would frame the investigators

### Clues the Party Missed

If the party had searched Vex's body more thoroughly (watch confiscated it too quickly), they would have found:
- The signet ring with hidden compartment and note
- Bruising on Vex's left wrist consistent with a recent struggle (he fought with someone the day before)
- A torn piece of fabric under his fingernails (from his assistant's sleeve)

These clues are now in watch custody, where The Architect's agents can destroy them.

### Future Revelations

Vex left a dead man's switch: a sealed letter with a neutral third party (the proprietor of The Brass Scales Inn) with instructions to deliver it to "whoever investigates my death" 48 hours after his demise.

The letter contains:
- Vex's confession to embezzlement (he wanted to clear his conscience)
- His suspicion that Councilor Thrane Silvermark is The Architect
- The cipher key for all coded communications
- An apology to the investigators for not being brave enough to help them when it mattered

This letter will be delivered to the party in 2 in-game days, assuming they survive that long.
